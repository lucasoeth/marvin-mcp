## Work Schedule

- **Primary work hours:** 9am-6pm EST
- **Peak productivity:** 9-11am (protect for priority 3/red tasks)
- **Low energy period:** 2-3pm (schedule admin, emails, light tasks)
- **No meeting days:** Tuesdays (deep work day)

## Task Management Preferences

- **Time blocks:** 90 minutes for deep work, 30 minutes for admin/email
- **Daily capacity:** Max 3-4 tasks, 6 hours total focused work
- **Priority usage:**
  - Red (3): Urgent client deadlines, must finish today
  - Orange (2): Important but flexible by 1-2 days
  - Yellow (1): Nice-to-have, can defer if needed
- **Planning style:** Plan day the night before, adjust in morning

## Energy & Productivity Patterns

- Most creative/focused: Morning (9-11am)
- Good for meetings: Late morning/early afternoon (11am-1pm)
- Afternoon slump: 2-3pm (avoid scheduling demanding work)
- Second wind: 4-5pm (good for finishing tasks started earlier)

## Marvin Project Structure

- **Work** (parentId: abc123): Client projects and deliverables
- **Personal** (parentId: def456): Side projects, learning, admin
- **Health** (parentId: ghi789): Exercise, meal prep, appointments
- **Content** (parentId: jkl012): Writing, social media, newsletters

## Recurring Commitments

- Team standup: Mon/Wed/Fri at 10am (30 min)
- One-on-ones: Thursday afternoons
- Weekly planning session: Sunday evening (60 min)
- Exercise: Mon/Wed/Fri at 7am (before work)

## Decision Frameworks

- **Client work always takes priority** over internal projects
- **Health commitments are non-negotiable** (exercise, sleep)
- **If overwhelmed:** Defer everything except red priority tasks
- **Inbox processing:** Weekly on Friday afternoons

## Communication Preferences

- **Be direct:** No fluff, get to the point
- **Ask specific questions:** "Which task moves to tomorrow?" not "How do you feel?"
- **Celebrate wins briefly:** "Done! Next?" is perfect
- **Challenge overcommitment:** Push back if I schedule >6 hours

## Things to Remember
